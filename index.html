Shipping & returns

Ordering and payment

Place your order from your bag and pay securely online. We prepare your order once payment is confirmed.

Delivery

Each piece is sent to you directly from our supplier. Delivery usually takes 1 to 4 weeks, depending on where you are. We share your tracking number as soon as your order ships. Delivery times are estimates and can be affected by customs and carriers.

Shipping costs and import duties

Shipping is set by region and shown in your bag before you order. It is one fee for the first item plus a smaller fee for each extra item, where we have set one. We ship worldwide. Depending on your country, customs may charge import duties or taxes when your parcel arrives. These are not included in our prices and are your responsibility.

Returns and refunds

If your order arrives damaged, faulty or not as described, email varele@varele.shop within 7 days of delivery with your order details and photos. We will replace it or refund you. Because pieces ship from overseas, we cannot accept returns for change of mind.
